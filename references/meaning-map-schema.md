# meaning-map.md schema

One section per post, one block per unit. Keep it terse: this is a working file, not prose.

```markdown
## P1 [x]
**Job:** drive clicks to the X1 SSD product page by tying a common frustration to storage.
**Rhythm:** hook (2 lines) / blank / product + proof / blank / CTA + URL / blank / hashtags. Emoji: 🤔 end of hook, 🎮 end of proof.

### U1 — hook
- Source: "Ever wondered why your laptop slows down after a year? 🤔"
- Function: recognition hook. The reader identifies with the problem.
- Payload: laptops tend to slow down after about a year of use; there is a reason for it.
- Effect: "yes, that's happening to me, why?"
- English framing: #4 "Ever wondered…", #1 "your"
- Vocab to keep close: laptop, slow down, (a) year
- Invariants: 🤔
- JP strategy (suggested): a situational question ending in 〜ませんか？; drop the 2nd person.

### U2 — reframe
- Source: "It's not you — it's your storage."
- Function: removes blame and names the real cause, which sets up the product.
- Payload: the slowdown isn't the user's fault/usage; storage is the cause.
- Effect: relief, plus curiosity about storage.
- English framing: #19 "It's not X — it's Y", em dash, #1 "your"
- Vocab to keep close: storage
- Invariants: —
- JP strategy: 〜ではなく + 原因は…; answer the "why" from U1.

(… U3, U4 …)

### Ambiguities
- none / "U3 'up to 7,300MB/s' — read speed only; do not imply write speed"
```

Unit function vocabulary: `hook`, `setup`, `reframe`, `claim`, `proof`, `benefit`, `offer`, `cta`, `link`, `signoff`, `disclosure`, `hashtags`, `thread-marker`, `emoji-only`.
